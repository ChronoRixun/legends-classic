# Legends Classic 0.2.4 candidate

Builder 0.2.4 / content 22 / catalogue 7 / LC31 requires XML2 Fix >=1.7.
The Toad map change can reset an older saved record for that encounter.
The save schema is unchanged.

## Integration gates

Both candidates passed 720 builder unit tests, both repository content guards and
31/31 guard self-tests, 114 non-startup native test invocations and 70/70 startup
cases. Full source and frozen game builds completed with zero errors; each
verified all 21,591 manifest files. All 21,592 non-metadata output files, including
the generated INI, match by SHA-256. Existing validation warnings remain recorded.

The combined startup write audit attributes all 40 bytes at 0x5a8dec..0x5a8e14
solely to the resistance adapter. The only shared writer is the existing display
and window-title IAT chain. Windowed, fullscreen, no-display and TestMode variants
pass, with production-DLL comparisons. Later device/heap hooks and unchanged
bytes within patch spans are outside that changed-byte audit.

A fresh build of released XML2 Fix 1.6.0 supplied the OFF comparison: the final
40-byte HUD loop is identical when ResistanceDisplay is absent or xml2. All
108-byte protocol-4 gameplay records match with the setting OFF and ON; the
existing 24 rules are unchanged. Focused production-installer tests also verify
no OFF writes and preservation of all seven native formatter calls when ON.

## Runtime acceptance

The assisted four-hero Toad check destroyed all four jets through ordinary
attacks, observed protected boss phases and four elevator soldiers, completed
the final fight, and settled in the mansion with the escort alive. Staging
included level ten, protection, map entry, checked floor recovery and facing;
this does not establish unassisted play or cinematic playback fidelity.

Live target-state traces and captures confirm physical subtitles on the Officer
and Revolutionary, energy on Pyro, mental on the Master, and no subtitle on a
non-resistant soldier. A vulnerable-party check observed one nonlethal gunman
firing-to-pain transition. Three menu quits exited cleanly.

**Release acceptance is blocked by co-op.** Two instances loaded saved campaign
slot zero, but twelve paired movement samples over 62.7 seconds failed the sync
check (maximum party position difference 460.4; settled difference 272.4).
No receive-validation drops were logged in that session. Area change was not
established. A controlled retry was stopped at the task limit before acceptance.
The cause is unresolved; no new gameplay rule or speculative engine fix was
added. Two-PC networking and every enemy modifier remain unproven.
